FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Until free?

Yes. It is MIT-licensed open source, with no account and no subscription.

Does my calendar data go anywhere?

No. There is no Until server. Mac calendars are read on your Mac, Google data goes straight from Google to your Mac, and sign-in tokens stay in the macOS Keychain. Privacy policy

Do I need a Google account?

No. Calendars on this Mac covers iCloud, Google, Outlook/Exchange, and CalDAV without signing in. Sign in with Google only for meeting notes docs and Meet links.

Does it work with Outlook or Microsoft 365?

Yes — add the account to the Calendar app (System Settings → Internet Accounts) and turn on Calendars on this Mac.

Which Macs?

macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel. Updates install automatically.

Private by design.

Until runs entirely on your Mac and has no servers and no account with us. Calendar app events are read locally; Google data goes straight from Google to your Mac, tokens stay in the macOS Keychain, and Drive access is limited to files Until created (drive.file scope). Full privacy policy →
